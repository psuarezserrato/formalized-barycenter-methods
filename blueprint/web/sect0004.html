Theorem 4.1 Global BCG volume-entropy integration and scale limit \(s \searrow h(X)\)
✓

  • BCG.bcg_volume_entropy_inequality

Let \((X, \mu _X)\) be a finite-measure space. If at every scale \(s {\gt} h_X\), the Jacobian \(J_s(x) = |\det d_x F_s|\) is integrable, bounded \(\mu _X\)-almost everywhere by \((s/h_Y)^n\), and satisfies the degree-area inequality \(|\deg (f)|\cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le \int _X J_s(x)\, d\mu _X(x)\), then

\[ |\deg (f)| \cdot h_Y^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le s^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(X) \quad (\forall s {\gt} h_X) \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad |\deg (f)| \cdot h_Y^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le h_X^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(X). \]
Proof ▶

Monotonicity of Bochner integration on \((X, \mu _X)\) followed by right-continuity of \(s \mapsto s^n \operatorname {Vol}(X)\) along the punctured neighborhood filter \(\mathcal{N}_{{\gt}}(h_X)\).

For the explicit smooth natural map \(F_s(x) = \mathrm{hyperboloidBarycenter}(w(s, x), \xi )\) on an \(n\)-dimensional hyperbolic target (\(n \ge 1\)), integrating \(|\det D^{\mathrm{Riem}} F_s(x)| \le (s/n)^n\) and passing to \(s \searrow c_X\) yields

\[ |\deg (f)| \cdot n^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le c_X^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(X). \]

Moreover, if equality holds at scale \(s {\gt} 0\), then for \(\mu _X\)-almost every \(x \in X\), \(|\det D^{\mathrm{Riem}} F_s(x)| = (s/n)^n\), both Busemann covariance tensors are isotropic (\(\frac{1}{n}\operatorname {id}_E\)), and \(D^{\mathrm{Riem}} F_s(x)\) is a \((s/n)\)-scaled linear isometry.

Proof ▶

Synthesizes theorem 2.8, theorem 2.9, and theorem 4.1.

\[ |\deg (f)| \cdot h_0^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le c_X^n \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(X), \qquad |\deg (f)| \cdot 22^{16} \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(Y) \le c_X^{16} \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(X). \]
Proof ▶

Combines theorem 3.2, theorem 3.5, and theorem 3.9 with theorem 4.1.
