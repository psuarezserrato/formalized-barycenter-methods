Definition 2.3Busemann covariance and cross-operators

For vectors \(u, u_1, u_2 \in E\), define the rank-one operators \((u \otimes u^*)(v) = \langle u, v\rangle u\) and \((u_1 \otimes u_2^*)(v) = \langle u_1, v\rangle u_2\). \[ H(w, u) = \sum _{a \in s} w_a (u_a \otimes u_a^*), \qquad L_c(w, u_1, u_2) = c \sum _{a \in s} w_a (u_{1,a} \otimes u_{2,a}^*). \]

Definition 1.5Exponential distance weights and critical exponent

\[ h(X, \mu , x_0) = \inf \left\{ s \in \mathbb {R}\; \middle |\; \int _X e^{-s d(x_0, z)}\, d\mu (z) {\lt} \infty \right\} , \]
