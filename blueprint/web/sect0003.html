For a unit Busemann gradient \(u \in E\) and orthogonal projection \(\pi _u\) onto the \(d\)-dimensional \(\mathbb {K}\)-line \(\mathbb {K}u\), the single-direction Busemann Hessian is \(W(u, \pi _u) = I - (u \otimes u^*) - (\pi _u - u \otimes u^*) = I - 2(u \otimes u^*) + \pi _u\), and the integrated Hessian operator is

\[ W_y = I - 2H + \widehat{H} = (I - H) + (\widehat{H} - H), \qquad \widehat{H} = \sum _{a \in s} w_a \pi _{u_a}. \]

Every \(\mathbb {K}\)-line Busemann Hessian satisfies the Riccati equation \(W(u, \pi _u)^2 = -R(\cdot , u)u = 4(\pi _u - u \otimes u^*) + (I - \pi _u)\) and \(\operatorname {Tr}(W(u, \pi _u)) = n + d - 2\). Moreover, \(\widehat{H} - H \ge 0\) with \(\operatorname {Tr}(\widehat{H}) = d\), and whenever \(W_y \circ D_F = s H_y\),

\[ |\det D_F| \le \left(\frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}\right)^n \frac{\sqrt{\det H}}{\det (I - 2H + \widehat{H})}. \]

Consequently, the rank-one determinant inequality \(\frac{\sqrt{\det H}}{\det (I - 2H + \widehat{H})} \le \left(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{h_0}\right)^n\) implies \(|\det D_F| \le (s/h_0)^n\), and the quantitative eigenvalue-defect version implies isotropy \(H = \frac{1}{n}\operatorname {id}_E\) upon equality.

Proof ▶

Orthogonal decomposition into \(\mathbb {R}u \oplus (\mathbb {K}u \ominus \mathbb {R}u) \oplus (\mathbb {K}u)^\perp \) with eigenvalues \((0, 2, 1)\), combined with Hölder/Cauchy–Schwarz on \(H_y\).

For any symmetric positive-semidefinite operators \(A, B : E \to _l[\mathbb {R}] E\) on an \(n\)-dimensional real inner product space and weights \(p, q \ge 0\) with \(p + q = 1\),

\[ \det (p \bullet A + q \bullet B) \ge (\det A)^p (\det B)^q. \]

By induction, if \(A_0, \dots , A_{m-1}\) are symmetric positive-semidefinite with \(\det A_r \ge d_0 \ge 0\), then \(\det \left(\frac{1}{m}\sum _{r=0}^{m-1} A_r\right) \ge d_0\).

Proof ▶

Simultaneous diagonalization via \(A^{-1/2}\) (\(D A D = \operatorname {id}_E\)), reducing to \(\prod _{i=1}^n (p + q \mu _i) \ge \prod _{i=1}^n \mu _i^q\) for the eigenvalues \(\mu _i \ge 0\) of \(D B D\).

For an associative IsOrthogonalKStructure d J (\(d \in \{ 2, 4\} \)):

  1. Property \(\mathrm{(P2)}\) holds globally: \(\widehat{H} - H = \sum _{r \ne 0} J_r H J_r^*\), and hence \(\det ((\widehat{H} - H)/(d - 1)) \ge \det H\) (BCG Lemma B.3).

  2. Combining the log-concavity splitting \(W_y = (n+d-2)\bigl(p\frac{I-H}{n-1} + q\frac{\widehat{H}-H}{d-1}\bigr)\) (\(p = \frac{n-1}{n+d-2}\), \(q = \frac{d-1}{n+d-2}\)) with BCG Lemma B.4 yields

    \[ \frac{\sqrt{\det H}}{\det (I - 2H + \widehat{H})} \le \left(\frac{\sqrt{n}}{n + d - 2}\right)^n \quad \text{and}\quad |\det D_F| \le \left(\frac{s}{n + d - 2}\right)^n. \]
Proof ▶

Applies theorem 3.3 to the \(d - 1\) orthogonal conjugates \(J_r H J_r^*\) (each having determinant \(\det H\)) and combines with the spectral AM–GM bound for \((I - H)/(n - 1)\).

In Ruan’s two-case analysis of the \(8 \times 8\) block decomposition \(H = \begin{pmatrix} A & C \\ C^* & B \end{pmatrix}\):

  1. Case 1 (\(2\lambda _1 \le \lambda \), \(2\mu _1 \le \mu \), \(\alpha = 0\)): For \(0 \le w_i \le h_{2,i} \le h_{1,i}\), \(\prod _i (h_{2,i} - w_i)\prod _i h_{1,i} \le \prod _i (h_{1,i} - w_i)\prod _i h_{2,i}\) (Ruan Lemma 3.5). Combined with \(\det (\lambda I_8 - A) \ge 7^8 \det A\) and \(\det (\mu I_8 - B) \ge 7^8 \det B\), Schur complement factorization yields \(\det (\widehat{H} - H) \ge 7^{16} \det H\).

  2. Case 2 (\(2\mu _1 {\gt} \mu \) or \(2\lambda _1 {\gt} \lambda \), \(\alpha \in [1 - \sqrt{2/3}, 0.266]\)): Every non-negative \(x : \mathrm{Fin}\, 8 \to \mathbb {R}\) satisfies the \(8\)-dimensional reverse AM–GM inequality \(7^7 \prod _{i=0}^7 x_i \le (\sum _{i=0}^7 x_i - x_0)^7 x_0\) (Ruan Lemma 3.6), and \(t = 1 - (1 - \alpha )^2\) satisfies \(t^2(1 - t) \ge 7/64\), yielding the cubic endpoint bound \(\frac{7}{64}\mu (\mu - \mu _1)\mu _1 \le (\mu - t\mu )(t\mu )^2\).

Proof ▶

Pointwise algebraic monotonicity for Case 1 and \(7\)-variable weighted AM–GM on \(\mathrm{Fin}\, 8 \setminus \{ 0\} \) for Case 2.

For any off-diagonal perturbation operator \(U_\alpha : E \to _l[\mathbb {R}] E\) such that \(I - H - U_\alpha \ge 0\) and \(\widehat{H} - H + U_\alpha \ge 0\), the Hessian operator splits as \(I - 2H + \widehat{H} = (I - H - U_\alpha ) + (\widehat{H} - H + U_\alpha )\) and satisfies Ruan’s perturbed log-concavity bound (Eq. (22)):

\[ \det (I - 2H + \widehat{H}) \ge 22^{16} \det \left(\frac{I - H - U_\alpha }{15}\right)^{15/22} \det \left(\frac{\widehat{H} - H + U_\alpha }{7}\right)^{7/22}. \]

Combined with Ruan Lemma 3.4 (Eqs. (21) and (23)), this proves Proposition 3.1 and Corollary 3.8 on \(\mathbb {O}\mathbf{H}^2\):

\[ \frac{\sqrt{\det H}}{\det (I - 2H + \widehat{H})} \le \left(\frac{\sqrt{16}}{22}\right)^{16} = \left(\frac{2}{11}\right)^{16} \quad \text{and}\quad |\det D_F| \le \left(\frac{s}{22}\right)^{16}. \]
Proof ▶

Applies theorem 3.3 with \(p = 15/22, q = 7/22\) to \((I - H - U_\alpha )/15\) and \((\widehat{H} - H + U_\alpha )/7\), then combines exponents \(\frac{4}{15}\cdot \frac{15}{22} + \frac{7}{22} = \frac{11}{22} = \frac{1}{2}\).
