On a metric-measure universal cover \((\widetilde{X}, \mathsf{d}, \mu )\), the logarithmic ball-volume rate \(\frac{1}{R}\log \mu (B(x_0, R))\) is basepoint-independent, bounded under \(\mathsf{RCD}(-(N-1)\kappa ^2, N)\) Bishop–Gromov comparison by the hyperbolic growth rate \((N-1)\kappa \), and yields \(\pi _1(X)\)-equivariant normalized exponential weights \(w_s(x, z)\). Moreover, the entropy-volume product \(h(\widetilde{X})^N \mathcal{H}^N(X)\) is strictly invariant under homothetic metric rescaling \((\mathsf{d}, \mathcal{H}^N) \mapsto (\lambda \mathsf{d}, \lambda ^N \mathcal{H}^N)\), and the entropy-degree inequality reduces across any \(k\)-sheeted orientation cover \(\widehat{X} \to X\) via \(|\deg (\hat{f})| = k|\deg (f)|\) and \(\mathcal{H}^N(\widehat{X}) = k\mathcal{H}^N(X)\).

Proof ▶

Triangle-inequality ball inclusion \(B(x_1, R) \subseteq B(x_0, R + \mathsf{d}(x_0, x_1))\), direct hyperbolic density estimate \(\sinh (\kappa r)^{N-1} \le (2\kappa )^{-(N-1)} e^{(N-1)\kappa r}\), and scaling laws \(h_\lambda = \lambda ^{-1}h\), \(V_\lambda = \lambda ^N V\).

Proof ▶

On the full-measure regular set \(\mathcal{R}_N(X)\) where \(\mathrm{Tan}(X, x) \cong \mathbb {R}^N\), the approximate linear differential \(d_x F_s : \mathbb {R}^N \to \mathbb {R}^N\) has metric Jacobian \(\mathrm{Jac}_x F_s = |\det (d_x F_s)|\) (Lemma 3.2). Since the orientation-sign sum on each regular fiber satisfies \(|\sum _{x \in F_s^{-1}(y) \cap \mathcal{R}_N(X)} \mathrm{sign}(\det d_x F_s)| \le \mathrm{mult}_{F_s}(y)\), the rectifiable area formula yields

\[ |\deg (f)| \, \mathcal{H}^N(Y) = \mathbf{M}(F_{s\# } T) \le \int _Y \mathrm{mult}_{F_s}(y)\, d\mathcal{H}^N(y) = \int _X \mathrm{Jac}_x F_s \, d\mathcal{H}^N(x) \]

(Lemma 3.3), and the rank-one Busemann Hessian estimate gives \(\mathrm{Jac}_x F_s \le (s / h(\widetilde{Y}))^N\) \(\mathcal{H}^N\)-almost everywhere on \(X\) (Proposition 3.4).

Proof ▶

Finite-fiber triangle inequality \(|\sum \sigma (x)| \le \# S\), Bochner integral monotonicity on \((Y, \mathcal{H}^N)\), and theorem 3.2 on \(\mathrm{Tan}(X, x) \cong \mathbb {R}^N\).

Let \((X, \mathsf{d}, \mathcal{H}^N)\) be a closed orientable non-collapsed \(\mathsf{RCD}(K, N)\) space (or a closed orientable \(N\)-dimensional Alexandrov space without boundary, Remark 4.3) and \(f : X \to Y\) a continuous map to a closed oriented negatively curved locally symmetric \(N\)-manifold \((Y, g_Y)\). Then:

\[ |\deg (f)| \, h(\widetilde{Y})^N \, \mathcal{H}^N(Y) \le h(\overline{X})^N \, \mathcal{H}^N(X). \]

Furthermore, if \(\deg (f) \ne 0\) and equality holds, then after homothetic normalization \(h(\overline{X}) = h(\widetilde{Y})\) and passing to the \(|\deg (f)|\)-sheeted cover \(\widehat{Y} \to Y\) where \(|\deg (\hat{F}_\infty )| = 1\) and \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) = \mathcal{H}^N(\widehat{Y})\), the \(1\)-Lipschitz limit map \(\hat{F}_\infty : X \to \widehat{Y}\) satisfies \(\mathrm{Jac}_x \hat{F}_\infty = 1\) and \(d_x \hat{F}_\infty \in O(N)\) \(\mathcal{H}^N\)-a.e., promotes to an isometric homeomorphism \(X \to \widehat{Y}\), and forces every point of \(X\) to be regular (\(\Sigma (X) = X \setminus \mathcal{R}_N(X) = \emptyset \)), so \(X\) is a smooth Riemannian manifold isometric to \(\widehat{Y}\) and \(f\) is homotopic to a Riemannian covering of degree \(|\deg (f)|\) (Suárez-Serrato (2026), Theorem 1.1).

Proof ▶

Combines theorem 5.6 and theorem 4.1 for the inequality; for equality, applies the mass-Jacobian sandwich \(\mathcal{H}^N(\widehat{Y}) = \mathbf{M}(\hat{F}_{\infty \# }T) \le \int _X \mathrm{Jac}_x \hat{F}_\infty \, d\mathcal{H}^N \le \mathcal{H}^N(X) = \mathcal{H}^N(\widehat{Y})\) to obtain \(\mathrm{Jac}_x \hat{F}_\infty = 1\) a.e., upgrades \(\hat{F}_\infty \) to an isometry onto \(\widehat{Y}\), and uses the Bishop–Gromov density gap \(\Theta ^N(\mathcal{H}^N, x) {\lt} 1\) on \(X \setminus \mathcal{R}_N(X)\) (theorem 5.3) to deduce \(\Sigma (X) = \emptyset \).
